How It Works

From Excel to audit evidence in 4 steps

No connectors. No consultants. No 6-month implementation.

1

Upload your data

Drag & drop your Excel with roles, approvers, and permission details. The app auto-detects columns and maps relationships.

2

Approvers review

Each manager sees only their assigned roles. They view every permission, acknowledge, and decide: Keep, Modify, or Reject.

3

Track progress

Real-time dashboard shows who certified, who's overdue, and where SoD conflicts exist. Send reminders with one click.

4

Export evidence

Generate signed PDF packages with full audit trails. Share with external auditors or archive for your next compliance assessment.
